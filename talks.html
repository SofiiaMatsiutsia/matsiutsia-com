Talks & workshops

Over the past year I've trained 500+ non-technical people in AI literacy — and learned that most AI adoption strategies fail because they ignore the human side. I speak about the barriers people actually face (fear, misconceptions, skill gaps), overcoming resistance to adoption, psychological safety around AI, and peer-to-peer learning models. I combine grassroots community experience with product design thinking to help people, teams, and orgs move from resistance to adoption.

I'm a product designer specializing in user research and human-centered design, and founder of Vibe Coding Collective.
